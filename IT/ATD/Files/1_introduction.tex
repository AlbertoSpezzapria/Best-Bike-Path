\section{Purpose}
This document reports the results of the Acceptance Testing phase performed on the \textbf{BestBikePaths (BBP)} application. The goal is to validate the software against the requirements specified in the RASD, verify the consistency of the architectural design, and assess the reproducibility of the installation process.

\section{Reviwed Project Identification}
